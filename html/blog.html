Featured Analysis Corporate Law 07 September 2026

What boards should consider before entering a major transaction.

The decisions that shape a transaction are usually made before the term sheet is signed. A practical framework for directors as they approach consequential matters.

Board approval is often treated as a formality at the end of a transaction. In practice, the most important decisions — the commercial thesis, the structure, the appetite for risk — are formed long before. This analysis sets out the questions a board should be asking itself well before the first meeting.
