Before You Contact Us

A few notes before you write.

These are the points clients most often ask about before the first conversation. Reading them will help you prepare a useful first enquiry.

What to include in your first message

A short description of the matter, the parties involved, the jurisdiction and any deadlines you are aware of. Do not send confidential documents until a formal engagement is in place.

Conflicts of interest

Before we can accept an engagement, we run a conflict check. If there is a conflict, we will tell you promptly and, where possible, suggest alternative counsel.

Fees and engagement

Fee arrangements are agreed in writing before work begins. Nothing on this website constitutes a fee quote or an offer to act.

No legal advice by email

Correspondence before a formal engagement is not legal advice, and does not create a lawyer-client relationship. Information on this website is general information only.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions

How quickly will I hear back after submitting an enquiry?

We respond to all enquiries within one business day. If your matter is urgent, please indicate that clearly in your message or call the office directly.

Is the first conversation free of charge?

Initial conversations to understand the matter and confirm whether we are the right firm to advise on it are not charged. Any work beyond that is agreed in writing before it begins.

Do you advise clients outside Tamil Nadu?

Yes. Our principal office is in Salem, but we advise clients across India and coordinate cross-border work in Asia, the Middle East, Europe and North America.

Can I send confidential documents with my first message?

We recommend waiting until a formal engagement is in place. A short, non-confidential description of the matter is sufficient for us to run conflict checks and assess fit.

How are fees structured?

Depending on the matter, we work on hourly, fixed-fee or retainer arrangements. The appropriate structure is discussed and agreed in writing before work begins.

Do you work with in-house legal teams?

Frequently. Many of our engagements are alongside in-house counsel — we take on specialist work, capacity support, or jurisdictional coordination, and coordinate closely with the client's team.

What if my matter does not fit a listed practice area?

Many matters sit between practice areas. Describe the situation in the form and we will either identify the right team internally or suggest alternative counsel.
